01 // about

The full stack of data.

Most projects break at the seams — between the data team, the model and the app. I work across all of them.

I'm a data & AI engineer who enjoys the whole journey: collecting and cleaning data, building reliable pipelines, exploring it to find the signal, training models that actually generalise, and shipping them inside clean, fast web and mobile interfaces.

From computer-vision systems running in real time to predictive models served as interactive apps, I care about results you can measure and products people enjoy using.
